Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Shree Pharma ("we", "us") is a supplier and exporter of Bacteriostatic Water for Injection USP, based in India. This policy explains what information we handle and why. Contact us at shreepharma.in@gmail.com with any question.

Information we collect

  • Quote requests: the name, company, business email, phone number, country, quantity and message you enter in the form on this website.
  • Business contact details: names, company names, business email addresses, phone numbers and countries of businesses that may buy our product, used for business-to-business outreach.
  • Email correspondence: emails you send us and our replies.

We do not sell products to individuals, and we do not knowingly collect information about children.

How we use it

  • To reply to quote requests and supply our product.
  • To send business emails about our product, including one follow-up, and to stop emailing anyone who asks.
  • To keep records of our business correspondence.

We never sell or rent your information, and we do not share it with third parties for their marketing.

Gmail and Google user data

Our private email dashboard connects to our own Gmail account through Google's API to create email drafts, send emails we approve, and detect replies, bounces and unsubscribe requests to those emails. It is used only by us, for our own mailbox.

Shree Pharma's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use Google user data for advertising, and we do not use it to train artificial intelligence models.

Where it is stored

Information is stored in our Gmail account (Google) and in our private dashboard hosted on Cloudflare. Access is protected by passwords and limited to us.

How long we keep it

We keep business contact details and correspondence for as long as needed for our business relationship and legal records. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

Your choices

  • To stop receiving our emails, reply "unsubscribe" or use the unsubscribe link in our email. We stop immediately.
  • To see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, email shreepharma.in@gmail.com.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
